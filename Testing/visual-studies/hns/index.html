Does newer Kanto art improve Fuchsia?

Recommendation: keep the current direction.

Heart & Soul gives Fuchsia darker cobbled streets, blue flowers, and tiny conifers. The buildings change very little, while the dark paving draws attention away from them. World’s light streets read more clearly. If you prefer the donor’s mood, the next step would be a separate Fuchsia-only port proposal.

Matched source-rendered concepts, not emulator screenshots. Both sides use World’s exact map layout, daytime colors, and a fixed animation frame. Actors, weather, reflections, and moving doors are omitted. The donor concept retains World’s water, sandy zoo floors, and unchanged art. Click any image for native pixels.

1. Pokémon Center approach

The heavier path and finer cliff texture are the main changes. The Pokémon Center remains almost the same.

2. Zoo-facing path

Cooler blue flowers are a useful alternative. The path’s strong contrast becomes the scene’s most prominent feature.

3. Residential boundary

Small conifers replace round shrubs. The houses remain familiar, so this feels like a landscaping change more than a town-wide facelift.

What the study settled

There are 232 explicit metatile mappings. Of 123 selected donor metatiles, 54 already have exact static matches in World’s Johto art. Newer source art does not necessarily mean new artwork for this project.

The concept mixes 14 unmerged palette rows; World’s map loader uses 13. A port needs deliberate palette packing and animation/door integration, not a sheet swap. Donor credits name multiple upstream artists, but individual asset reuse terms remain unverified. New donor artwork is excluded from production until that is resolved.

# Pokédex and item guide

Every Pokémon and item you can get in **Pokémon World**, generated from the game data: base stats, Abilities and
evolutions are read from the built ROM, and locations from the wild tables, map events and scripts.

- **404 Pokémon** are obtainable: 383 of the 386 from Generations 1 to 3, plus 21 later-generation relatives of those lines.
- **435 items** have at least one source.

## How to read this

- **Stats** are HP / Attack / Defense / Sp. Atk / Sp. Def / Speed, with the total in brackets.
- **Found** lists places by region. `walk` is grass or cave floor, `surf` is water, rods are fishing, and `DexNav` is a
  hidden encounter that only the DexNav search finds (shown only where the Pokémon is not otherwise on that map).
- Wild tables do not change with the time of day.
- Link trading is not in the game. Trade evolutions use a **Linking Cord** or the trade item from the Bag, and
  only evolutions you can actually trigger are listed.
- Baby Pokémon with no wild source are bred at a Day Care. The later-generation babies need no incense.

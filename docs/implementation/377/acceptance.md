## Natural-play limits

These are automated controller transitions with prerequisite progress, IDs, items or party traits seeded where stated, plus production helper tests. They are not a fresh-save end-to-end natural campaign playthrough, 48 physical Center visits, physical-hardware testing, or a natural NPC/link-trade session. The forced-loss scenario sets battle outcome/HP to exercise actual whiteout recovery; it does not assess battle balance.
